TOWN OF FAIRFAX
ADOPTION NOTICE AND
SUMMARY OF ORDINANCE 900
AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF FAIRFAX AMENDING
CHAPTER 8.06 OF THE FAIRFAX TOWN CODE AND ADOPTING THE 2025 CALIFORNIA
WILDLAND-URBAN INTERFACE CODE, AND CERTAIN PORTIONS OF THE 2024
INTERNATIONAL WILDLAND-URBAN INTERFACE CODE, WITH CERTAIN LOCAL
AMENDMENTS AND ADDITIONS.
Notice is hereby given that on November 5, 2025, the Town Council of the Town of Fairfax
(“Town”) adopted Ordinance No. 900, amending Chapter 8.06 of the Fairfax Town Code.
Below is a summary of the ordinance:
This ordinance adopts the 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code and portions of the
2024 International version, with local amendments. It establishes updated standards for
construction, access, vegetation management, and fire protection in areas where development
meets wildland conditions. The revisions consolidate prior fire and building code sections
related to wildfire risk and home hardening. Local amendments require vegetation
management plans, emergency water supply maps, and coordination between building and fire
officials. The ordinance also strengthens requirements for road design, gates, and defensible
space around properties. The goal is to improve consistency and wildfire resilience throughout
the Town. The ordinance carries no fiscal impact and is exempt from CEQA.
The above is a simply a summary of the Ordinance. To obtain a full understanding of the
Ordinance, it should be read in its entirety. A certified copy of the full text of the Ordinance is
posted in the Town Clerk’s office at Fairfax Town Hall, 142 Bolinas Road, Fairfax, CA 94930
and online at www.townoffairfaxca.gov.
